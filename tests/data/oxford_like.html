abandon

verb BrE /əˈbændən/ NAmE /əˈbændən/
  1. 1~ sb to leave sb, especially sb you are responsible for, with no intention of returning 抛弃，遗弃（尤指不负责任）
    • The baby had been abandoned by its mother. 这个婴儿被母亲遗弃了。
    • People often simply abandon their pets when they go abroad. 人们出国时常常干脆把宠物丢下不管。
  2. 2to leave a thing or place, especially because it is impossible or dangerous to stay （不得已而）舍弃，丢弃，离开
    • They had to abandon the car and walk the rest of the way. 他们只好弃车步行剩下的路。
  3. 3to stop doing sth, especially before it is finished 中止；放弃；不再有
    • They abandoned the match because of rain. 因为下雨，他们中止了比赛。
